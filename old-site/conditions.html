01 / CONDITION

Piles

Piles can involve swelling or lumps (masses) coming out of the anus. Bleeding, discomfort and other symptoms need assessment because their causes can vary.

Signs people may notice

  • Swelling or a lump near or coming out of the anus
  • Discomfort or bleeding that should be checked
  • Symptoms that persist or change

How Ayurveda may help

A qualified practitioner can discuss Ayurvedic care and suitable products based on your symptoms and health history. Do not self-diagnose or delay an examination if advised.

02 / CONDITION

Fissure

A fissure is a tear or crack in the anal area that can cause pain, burning and bleeding, particularly during or after passing stool.

Signs people may notice

  • Pain or burning in the anal area
  • Bleeding that needs a clinician's assessment
  • Discomfort that does not settle or keeps returning

How Ayurveda may help

Discuss your symptoms with a qualified practitioner. They can explain whether Ayurvedic guidance is appropriate and whether examination or other care is needed.

03 / CONDITION

Fistula

A fistula may present as a small passage or boil near the anus, sometimes with sticky or pus-like discharge. It needs professional assessment.

Signs people may notice

  • A boil or opening near the anus
  • Sticky or pus-like discharge
  • Recurring irritation or pain

How Ayurveda may help

First, seek an appropriate medical assessment. A practitioner can help discuss next steps and whether any complementary Ayurvedic guidance is suitable for you.

04 / CONDITION · PROMPT CARE

Bhagandar

People may use this term for serious anal-area symptoms, including heavy bleeding, severe burning and pain, or tissue coming out. These symptoms require urgent attention.

When to seek help

  • Heavy bleeding or severe pain
  • Fever, worsening symptoms or feeling unwell
  • Any sudden or concerning change

How Ayurveda may help

Do not rely on self-treatment or a website description. Seek prompt medical care first; discuss complementary guidance only with qualified professionals.
