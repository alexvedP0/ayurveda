Everyday wellbeing

Gentle food and lifestyle considerations

General wellbeing habits may support comfort, but they do not replace diagnosis or treatment. Ask a qualified practitioner what is suitable for your health.

  • Drink fluids regularly, if appropriate for your health
  • Include a varied, fibre-containing diet as tolerated
  • Try to avoid straining and long periods on the toilet
  • Discuss persistent symptoms with a healthcare professional
  • Ask your clinician before making major diet or medicine changes
