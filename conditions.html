A common concern

Piles

Swelling or lumps (masses) that may come out of the anus. Symptoms can have different causes and may need an examination.

If you notice a lump, discomfort, or bleeding, speak with a qualified practitioner rather than self-diagnosing. Heavy bleeding or severe pain needs prompt medical attention.

Pain or burning

Fissure

A tear or crack in the anal area that can cause pain, burning and bleeding.

Anal pain and bleeding can have different causes. A practitioner can assess what is happening and advise on suitable next steps.

Needs assessment

Fistula

A small passage or boil near the anus, sometimes with sticky or pus-like discharge.

A suspected fistula should be assessed by a qualified medical practitioner. Do not rely on a website description to diagnose or choose treatment.

Please seek prompt care

Bhagandar

Heavy bleeding, severe burning and pain, or the anal area coming out can be serious symptoms.

Some people describe this as a fourth-level problem, but only a qualified practitioner can assess the cause and severity. Heavy bleeding, severe pain, or worsening symptoms need prompt medical attention—not self-treatment alone.
